# Home `/`

Mode: Persuade.

Audience: Saratoga Springs families, donors, partners. Job: understand the mission, see real local impact, donate, find the next event, ask a question.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a playground plot: safety-surfacing color fields and one oversized local crop carry mission and Donate on the first screen. It refuses a centered eyebrow-headline-paragraph-two-button hero and refuses three equal rounded impact cards.

OWN-WORLD: Sun gold field, field green proof, sky timetable, ink type, 0–6px rectangles. Bricolage Grotesque as park-sign display; Atkinson Hyperlegible as body. Photography at equipment scale, overlapping the field. Proof is inscribed year/amount/recipient type, not metric tiles.

STORY: Visitor reads the mission, sees Donate, then tangible support, featured projects, the next events, and a contact form. Empty CMS lists stay honest and unlabeled as facts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky compact header with wordmark, four links, filled Donate rectangle. Below: left/lower sun field with mission heading, one-sentence purpose, Donate; next event as a thin date+title strip on the field’s inner edge; majority crop of the hero photo overlapping the field. No glass overlay. Mobile: field (mission, Donate, next event) then full-bleed photo.

FORM: Playground Field (user-pinned, seed key none). Signature interaction: one field-and-crop settle on first paint; hover only on controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
