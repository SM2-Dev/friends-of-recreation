# Reference design DNA

Use the references as directional ingredients, not templates to copy. Do not reproduce proprietary layouts, illustrations, brand marks, copy, or interaction sequences.

## Toca Boca

Borrow confidence, playful scale, bold image and type relationships, and a willingness to break rigid grids. Translate the energy into real recreation photography and civic warmth. Do not imitate children's characters or make the organization feel like a toy brand.

## Maggie

Borrow editorial warmth, human storytelling, candid imagery, and irregular pacing. Keep navigation and core actions simpler than an experimental editorial site because donations, events, and forms must remain obvious.

## Lokal

Borrow mission clarity, people-centered impact, and strong presentation of meaningful proof. Use amounts, years, recipients, and beneficiaries as story material, not dashboard metrics.

## Ivy Kids

Borrow family-friendly usability, approachable content, trust, and practical conversion clarity. Avoid drifting toward ecommerce merchandising or repetitive product cards.

## Combined direction

Create an energetic community editorial system:

- authentic local photography at generous scale
- expressive but readable typography
- strong, irregular section rhythm
- clear civic purpose and tangible proof
- approachable donation, event, contact, and grant actions
- restrained playfulness suitable for adults, families, partners, and donors

The site should not look like a collage of four references. Choose a small set of compatible ideas and express them consistently through the Friends of Recreation brand.
